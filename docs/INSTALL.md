# Installing Stream Scheduler 2

This guide is for studio staff setting up the app on a station computer. No
technical background needed — just follow the numbered steps.

## Installing on a Mac

1. Download the DMG file (it will look like `Stream Scheduler 2-2.0.0-mac-universal.dmg`).
2. Double-click the DMG file to open it.
3. Drag the **Stream Scheduler 2** icon into the **Applications** folder.
4. **First launch only:** don't just double-click it — instead, right-click
   (or Control-click) the app in your Applications folder and choose **Open**.
5. A warning box will pop up saying the app is from an unidentified developer.
   Click **Open** on that box.
   - This is a one-time step. The app isn't digitally signed by Apple, so the
     Mac shows this warning the first time. Clicking Open tells your Mac you
     trust this app — it won't ask again.
   - If your Mac doesn't show an Open button in that box: go to **System
     Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the note about the
     blocked app, and click **Open Anyway**.
6. After this first time, the app opens normally — just double-click it like
   any other app.

## Installing on Windows

1. Download the installer file (it will look like
   `Stream Scheduler 2-2.0.0-win-x64.exe`).
2. Double-click the installer to run it.
3. Windows may show a blue box titled **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is
   expected — the app isn't signed with a Windows publisher certificate yet.
   - Click **More info**.
   - Then click **Run anyway**.
   - This is also a one-time step.
4. Follow the installer's prompts to finish installing.

## First-time setup in the app

The first time you open the app (Mac or Windows), it will ask you to set a
few things up:

1. Click the **Setup** button in the top-right corner of the app.
2. Pick the **slate picture** — the image shown on screen before the class
   starts.
3. Pick the **waiting music** — the music that plays under the slate
   (optional; you can skip this).
4. Choose the **streaming quality**.
5. Enter your **content-portal email and password** (the login you use for
   the Echelon content portal).
6. Click **Test login** and wait for the green checkmark — this confirms your
   login works before you save.
7. Click **Save & finish**.

That's it — the app is ready to schedule classes.

## If you see a banner about an update

If a banner appears saying a newer version is available, that's just a
notice — it doesn't update the app for you. Contact your admin so they can
install the new version on this computer.
